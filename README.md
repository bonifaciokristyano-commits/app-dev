# 🎬 Favorite Series Guide: Breaking Bad

> "I am not in danger, Skyler. I am the danger." — *Walter White*

## 📌 Overview
Breaking Bad is an American neo-Western crime drama television series created and produced by Vince Gilligan. Set and filmed in Albuquerque, New Mexico, the series tells the story of Walter White, an underpaid, overqualified, and dispirited high school chemistry teacher who is struggling with a recent diagnosis of stage-three lung cancer.

## 🎭 Main Characters
Here are some of the primary characters and their respective roles:
* **Walter White ("Heisenberg")** — High school chemistry teacher turned drug lord.
* **Jesse Pinkman** — Former student and Walter's business partner.
* **Skyler White** — Walter's wife.
* **Hank Schrader** — Walter's brother-in-law and a dedicated DEA agent.
* **Saul Goodman** — A flamboyant criminal lawyer.
* **Gustavo Fring** — A major narcotics distributor disguised as a fast-food entrepreneur.
